Clint Humphrey

Clint Humphrey is a wildlife photographer working with a Nikon Z 8 and a 150–600mm lens — the kind of long glass that turns a distant osprey into a portrait.

His work concentrates on the decisive moment: the dive, the strike, the impossible geometry of wings at full extension. Each frame is held to a fine-art standard.

Every print on this site is made from Clint's own original files. When you hang one of these, you're hanging the actual frame — not a stock photo, not a composite.
