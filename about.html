The photographer

Clint Humphrey

Clint Humphrey is a wildlife photographer working with a Nikon Z 8 and a 150–600mm lens — the kind of long glass that turns a distant osprey into a portrait.

His work concentrates on the decisive moment: the dive, the strike, the impossible geometry of wings at full extension. More recently the camera has turned toward American icons and roadside light — theme-park marquees at dusk, neon after dark, chrome under a hard sun — each frame held to the same fine-art standard.

Print quality

Made like it matters.

These are not posters off a warehouse shelf. Each print is produced to order from the original high-resolution file, by a professional print lab, and shipped directly to you.

Paper & finishes

  • Fine-art poster — heavyweight matte paper (200gsm), unframed. Ships rolled in a protective tube.
  • Framed print — the fine-art poster, hand-framed in black wood with a clean gallery profile. Arrives ready to hang.
  • Gallery canvas — the image wrapped on a slim 2cm stretcher frame. Arrives ready to hang, no frame needed.

Honest sizing

Each photograph is offered only in the sizes its resolution can genuinely support. A 45-megapixel frame can hold a 24×36 wall; a tight crop is offered smaller, where it stays razor sharp. What arrives is always sharp — that’s the whole point.

Shipping & promise

  • Printed to order — please allow a few days for production.
  • Shipped worldwide with tracking, packed to survive the journey.
  • Made to order — if your print arrives damaged or misprinted, contact us and we’ll make it right.
