Clint Humphrey

Portrait

Clint Humphrey is a wildlife photographer working with a Nikon Z 8 and a 150–600mm lens.

His work concentrates on the decisive moment: the dive, the strike, the geometry of wings at full extension. Each frame is held to a fine-art standard.

Every print on this site is made from Clint’s own original files — not stock photography, not composites.

How prints are made

Each print is produced to order from the original high-resolution file by a professional print lab, and shipped directly to you.

  • Fine-art poster — heavyweight matte paper (200gsm), unframed. Ships rolled in a protective tube.
  • Framed print — the fine-art poster, hand-framed in black wood. Arrives ready to hang.
  • Gallery canvas — the image wrapped on a slim 2cm stretcher frame. Arrives ready to hang.

Sizing

Each photograph is offered only in the sizes its resolution can genuinely support.

Shipping

  • Printed to order — please allow a few days for production.
  • Shipped worldwide with tracking.
  • If a print arrives damaged or misprinted, contact us and we’ll make it right.
